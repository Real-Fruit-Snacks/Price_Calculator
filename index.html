Why Us

Why Choose Danni's House

Personal, attentive care in a home setting, with pricing you can see up front.

Home Environment

Your dog stays in a comfortable home rather than a kennel, with cozy places to rest, room to play, and someone paying attention to them.

Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees or surprise charges. Every estimate shows a full breakdown: daily rate, extra hours, the multi-pet discount, and any holiday fee.

Instant Estimates

Choose your dates, add your pets, and see the full cost in seconds. Print the estimate or save the stay to your calendar.

Flexible Scheduling

Overnight stays, longer boarding, and flexible drop-off and pick-up times. Extra hours are billed by the hour and capped at the daily rate, so a partial day never costs more than a full one.

Personalized Care

Every dog gets attention suited to their needs and personality: their own food, their routine, and their favorite things from home.

Updates While You're Away

Care instructions and vet details are collected at drop-off, and you get regular updates with photos so you always know how your dog is doing.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the stay and the pricing.

Each 24-hour period from drop-off counts as one day at $55. For example, dropping off at 3pm and picking up at 3pm the next day is one day. Any extra time beyond a full day is charged at $5 per hour, rounded up to the next hour. Extra hours never cost more than a day: once they would add up to the daily rate (11 or more extra hours), the calculator counts another full day instead.
Feeding (using the food you provide), walks, playtime, companionship, a comfortable sleeping area, and regular updates with photos. Every dog is treated like part of the household.
The first pet pays full price, and each additional pet gets 20% off both the daily rate and any extra hours. For example, one pet for one day is $55, a second pet is $44, so two pets for one day come to $99.
Yes. If the stay includes a holiday, a 5% holiday fee is added on top of the total for the stay (after any multi-pet discount). For example, a $200 stay over a holiday comes to $210. Turn on "Holiday Stay" in the calculator to include it in your estimate.
Your pet's food, any medications, a favorite toy or blanket, and written care instructions including your vet's details. Anything specific to your pet's routine is worth mentioning at drop-off.
Yes. For stays of 3 or more days, a $50 deposit is due at booking. It holds your dates and counts toward the total, so the remaining balance is due at pick-up. The calculator shows the deposit and balance whenever a stay qualifies.
Yes. You'll get regular updates with photos and messages about your dog's activities, meals, and mood, so you always know how they're doing.
